// Overview

SILENTFALL is a CTI framework designed around a specific threat category that is notoriously difficult to detect: zero-click exploitation — attacks that require no user interaction and leave minimal forensic artifacts.

The framework maps known zero-click attack patterns (NSO Group Pegasus, FORCEDENTRY, BlastPass) to MITRE ATT&CK techniques, then engineers detection logic at the network and endpoint layer targeting the behavioral indicators that persist even when payload artifacts are cleaned.

"Zero-click attacks are undetectable by definition at the payload level. SILENTFALL detects them at the behavioral level — the C2 callouts, the anomalous process spawns, the impossible data flows."
