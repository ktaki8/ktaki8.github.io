Approach

My work integrates technical security engineering with institutional decision-making. I focus on designing defensive systems that remain functional under uncertainty, adversarial pressure, and organizational complexity.

As a Senior Fellow and Region Liaison at the Cyber Security Forum Initiative (CSFI), I work at the intersection of cybersecurity diplomacy and technical analysis — including capacity-building initiatives across the Middle East and Africa regions.

I translate deep technical analysis into actionable governance and policy recommendations — bridging the gap between what security engineers build and what decision-makers need to act on.

"Security that survives the real world must be built for uncertainty, not ideal conditions."
