// Overview

This tabletop exercise was designed to stress-test both the technical incident response capability and the governance decision-making of participants simultaneously — a gap that most standard IR exercises fail to address.

The scenario places a fictional water utility under a coordinated cyberattack targeting both IT corporate infrastructure and OT SCADA systems, forcing participants to navigate conflicting priorities between operational continuity, public safety, regulatory reporting, and law enforcement coordination.

"Real incidents break at the seam between technical teams and decision-makers. This exercise was designed to find and stress that seam under realistic pressure."

// Exercise Scenarios
INJECT 01
Initial Compromise — IT Network
Phishing → Lateral Movement
A spearphishing email compromises a billing department workstation. Threat actor establishes persistence and begins reconnaissance of internal systems.
  • →IT team must contain without disrupting billing operations
  • →Legal must assess breach notification requirements (state law + CISA)
INJECT 02
OT Network Pivot
IT→OT Lateral Movement
Attacker pivots from IT to the OT environment via a poorly segmented historian server, gaining read access to SCADA telemetry for the water treatment process.
  • →Operations must decide: isolate OT (lose visibility) or maintain connectivity (accept risk)
  • →Leadership must evaluate public disclosure timeline vs. ongoing investigation
INJECT 03
Chemical Control Manipulation Attempt
Physical Consequence Escalation
Alarm triggers on the chlorination system — anomalous commands detected. Unclear if attacker has write access or is testing defenses. Potential public safety impact.
  • →Executive decision required: notify public health authorities now or wait for confirmation?
  • →FBI and CISA notification procedures activate
  • →Manual override protocols and OT playbook validation
// Participant Roles
🔧 Technical Track
SOC analysts and OT engineers. Responsible for detection, containment, and forensic preservation decisions.
🏛️ Governance Track
Executives, legal, and communications. Responsible for regulatory reporting, public messaging, and resource authorization.
🤝 Coordination Track
Liaison roles bridging technical and governance tracks, managing information flow and prioritizing competing demands.
