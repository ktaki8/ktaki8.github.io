// Overview

Space Cyber Crew is an AI-driven threat intelligence pipeline designed to detect emerging cyber risks targeting satellite infrastructure and related communications systems.

The project collects open-source intelligence signals associated with ground stations, satellite networks, and critical cyber activity. Multiple AI agents then process, filter, and analyze these signals to identify patterns that may indicate elevated risk.

By combining OSINT collection with multi-agent analysis, Space Cyber Crew explores how automated intelligence workflows can support earlier detection, better prioritization, and analyst-ready reporting in space cybersecurity environments.

"Space cyber defense requires faster signal detection, smarter prioritization, and analyst-ready intelligence before weak signals escalate into mission-level risks."
