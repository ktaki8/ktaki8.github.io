// Overview

Space Cyber Brief Crew was designed to simulate an automated cyber threat intelligence pipeline for emerging risks affecting satellite infrastructure, space ground stations, and critical communications systems.

The system deploys multiple AI agents built with CrewAI to collect open-source intelligence, monitor cyber activity related to space infrastructure, and generate structured threat briefs to support analyst decision-making.

The project explores how autonomous AI agents can assist human analysts in detecting early signals of cyber threats targeting space systems and national infrastructure.
