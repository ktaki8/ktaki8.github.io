// Overview

Space Cyber Crew simulates an automated cyber threat intelligence pipeline focused on emerging risks affecting satellite infrastructure, ground stations, and critical communications systems.

The system deploys multiple AI agents built with CrewAI to collect open-source intelligence, monitor cyber activity related to space infrastructure, and generate structured threat intelligence briefs that support analyst decision-making.

By combining OSINT signals with automated analysis, the project explores how AI agents can assist cybersecurity teams in detecting early indicators of cyber threats targeting space systems and national infrastructure.

"Space cyber defense requires faster signal detection, smarter prioritization, and analyst-ready intelligence before weak signals escalate into mission-level risks."
